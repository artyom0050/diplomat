Privacy Policy

The Diplomat Junior, Glendora · Last updated: September 16, 2026

This page explains what information our online menu collects and how it is used.

What we collect

The menu itself does not ask for your name, email, or any other personal details, and it does not take orders or payments.

If you tap Accept on the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics and Google Ads. These services set cookies and receive information such as the pages and dishes you view, your device and browser type, approximate location, and an online identifier.

If you tap Decline, these services are not loaded.

How we use it

  • To understand how guests use the menu (for example, which dishes are viewed most).
  • To show ads for The Diplomat Junior to people who visited our menu, on Google and partner websites and apps (remarketing).

Google processes this information under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Your choices

  • Change your choice at any time:
  • Manage Google ad personalization: adssettings.google.com
  • Opt out of Google Analytics: Google Analytics opt-out add-on

California residents may have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to opt out of the sharing of personal information for targeted advertising. Declining cookies on the banner is how you opt out on this menu.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Please ask your server or contact The Diplomat Junior, Glendora.
